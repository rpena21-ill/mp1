About the Tournament

The 2026 FIFA World Cup marks the first tournament hosted jointly by three nations - the United States, Mexico, and Canada - and the first to feature an expanded 48-team format. Matches were played across 16 host cities from June 11 to July 19 2026.

Tournament Highlights

Opening Match

Mexico City kicks off the tournament at Estadio Azteca, with Raul Jimenez scoring for Mexico against South Africa.

Underdog Stories

There were many notable stories across the tournament, including breakout star Vozinha and his country Cape Verde.

The Final

MetLife Stadium hosted the culmination of the tournament with Ferran Torres and Spain besting Argentina.

Iconic Stadiums

From Estadio Azteca to MetLife Stadium, the 2026 tournament spanned some of the best venues in North America, with 16 host cities across 3 host nations.
